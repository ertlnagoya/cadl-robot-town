# The KPI of the service (an example that allows the regulation to be revised)

Used in §13 of Course D. Besides the rules and the contract, the LLM may propose amendments of
the regulation (the invariants and the quorum of the constitution). The regulation is decided by
the regulator, not by the operator. With --decider llm --delegate rule,contract,regulation, the
LLM decides in place of the operator and of the regulator.

## From period 1

Deliver as many orders as possible across the town within the times we promised customers. Leave
no company's customers behind. If the votes of the three companies or the conditions of the
constitution (the invariants and the quorum) stand in the way of amendments that are good for
customers, a revision of the regulation may be proposed. The regulation reaches all three
companies and the customers, so say who is affected and how.
