# The KPI of the service (an example that allows the contract to be revised)

Used in §13 of Course D. The LLM proposes amendments of the rules (how the roads are used) and
of the contract (the times promised to customers), and with --decider llm it decides in place of
the human.

## From period 1

Deliver as many orders as possible across the town within the times we promised customers. The
promised times (from order to pickup, and from pickup to delivery) may be revised if that is
better than keeping promises we cannot keep. A change that is worse for customers needs a reason,
given after measuring its effect on them.
